Understanding Dyscalculia

What is Dyscalculia?

Dyscalculia is a specific learning difference that affects the ability to understand, learn, and perform mathematical calculations. It impacts number sense, mathematical reasoning, and the application of mathematical concepts in daily life.

Common Signs

Difficulty with basic arithmetic, trouble understanding mathematical symbols, challenges with time management and money handling, anxiety around numbers, and difficulty remembering mathematical procedures or formulas.

Why Get Assessed?

Early identification enables access to appropriate accommodations, specialized support services, and learning strategies tailored to your needs, helping you succeed academically and build confidence with numbers.

Your Privacy

All assessment data is confidential and stored securely. You control whether your results are shared with academic support staff. Your participation is voluntary and will not affect your academic standing.
